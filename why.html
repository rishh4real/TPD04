WHY THE PROTEIN DROP?

A small
rebellion.

We have complicated food far too much. We count it. Fear it. Earn it. Regret it.

01 / THE BELIEF

Food needs to be loved, shared and empowered.

TPD is our small rebellion against all of that. Food should not arrive with a lecture, a spreadsheet, or an apology.

So, we make food from a position of love and care and ensure it is nourishing enough in terms of protein.

Good food and good health should not sit at opposite ends of the table. They should arrive together, hot, generous and easy to share.

THE REAL DROP
