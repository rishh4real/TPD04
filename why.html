01 / THE BELIEF

No guilt.
No fads.
No boring food.

The Protein Drop is deeply personal because it comes from a place many people know too well: loving food while being told to treat it with guilt, restraint, and regret.

I don't believe in punishment masquerading as health. I don't think good food and good health should live at opposite ends of the table.

Because life is busy. Because we are all trying to do better for our bodies, our health, and our families. And because somewhere along the way, healthy food became joyless and delicious food became sinful.

THE REAL DROP

without the lecture.

Your chicken can be smoky one day and fiery the next. Your fish can remind you of home. Your salad should never feel like an apology.
